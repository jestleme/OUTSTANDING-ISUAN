TOI Essays

In His Own Words

What is your most significant achievement as a student?

My most significant achievement as a student is becoming a leader who serves with purpose and genuine care for people. Throughout my years in leadership, I have always believed that leadership is not about titles, but about the impact you create in the lives of others.

During my time as a student leader, I hosted general assemblies, campus seminars, and provincial youth activities. What I value most is how I supported and empowered the members of the organizations I led — guiding fellow members, listening to their ideas, and helping them grow in confidence and responsibility.

My greatest contribution to ISU and the community is not just the projects I led, but the people I inspired and the values of leadership, service, and integrity that I strived to uphold.

How have you contributed to the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)?

Through my projects and advocacies, I have actively contributed to the SDGs by promoting inclusivity, youth empowerment, and environmental stewardship.

Establishing the LAKAPATI Club as SSC Committee Head aligns with SDG 5: Gender Equality and SDG 10: Reduced Inequalities. The three-day Linggo ng Kabataan I directed supports SDG 4: Quality Education and SDG 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities.

Environmental advocacy through Project Sangaribo and the 2nd Isabela Provincial Youth Parliament contributed to SDG 13: Climate Action and SDG 15: Life on Land, encouraging communities to take responsibility for a sustainable future.
